# ProjectPulse - Privacy Policy

**Version 1.0 · Effective March 1, 2026**

---

## Our Promise

Your data belongs to you. We collect only what's necessary, we never sell it, and we make it easy for you to access, export, or delete it. This Privacy Policy explains our practices in plain English.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### Information you provide
- **Account details:** Name, email address, username, timezone preference
- **Content you create:** Projects, tasks, comments, files, time entries, custom field data
- **Communications:** Messages you send us (support requests, feedback)

### Information collected automatically
- **Usage data:** Features you use, pages you visit, actions you take within the app
- **Performance data:** Error reports and service performance metrics

### Information we do NOT collect
- We do not collect biometric data
- We do not use third-party advertising trackers

## 2. How We Use Your Information

- **Provide the service:** Store your projects, display your data, enable collaboration
- **Improve the experience:** Understand usage patterns to make ProjectPulse better
- **Security:** Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access
- **Communications:** Send essential notifications (security alerts, service updates). Marketing emails are opt-in only — you choose whether to receive them

We process your data based on:
- **Contract performance** (providing the service you signed up for)
- **Legitimate interest** (security, service improvement)

## 3. Data Storage & Security

- **Encryption at rest and in transit:** Your data is encrypted using industry-standard AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.2+ in transit
- **Per-tenant isolation:** In multi-tenant deployments, your data is cryptographically isolated from other organizations
- **Access controls:** Only authorized personnel can access production systems, with audit trails
- **Regular security reviews:** We conduct periodic security assessments of our infrastructure
- **No indefinite storage:** We retain data only as long as needed (see Section 8)

## 4. Data Sharing

**We never sell your data. Period.**

We may share data only in these limited circumstances:

- **Service providers:** Essential infrastructure providers (hosting, email delivery) who are contractually bound to protect your data
- **With your consent:** Any other sharing only with your explicit permission

## 5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

- **Access** your data - see what we store about you
- **Export** your data - download it in standard formats (JSON/CSV)
- **Correct** inaccurate information
- **Restrict** processing in certain circumstances
- **Object** to processing based on legitimate interest
- **Withdraw consent** for optional processing at any time
- **Data portability** - receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format

To exercise these rights, contact us at **anubhav.sharma.work@outlook.com** 


## 6. Cookies & Tracking

- **Essential cookies only** by default — these are required for authentication and basic functionality
- **Optional analytics:** If we implement analytics, you'll be asked for consent first. You can opt out anytime
- **No third-party advertising trackers.** We don't display ads and don't allow ad networks to track you through our platform

## 7. Children's Privacy

- ProjectPulse is designed for users aged **13 and older** (COPPA compliant)
- We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13
- If you're a parent and believe your child has created an account, contact us and we'll promptly delete it
- In jurisdictions where a higher age of digital consent applies (e.g., 16 in some EU countries), that local age requirement applies

## 8. Data Retention

- **Active accounts:** We keep your data for as long as your account is active
- **Backups:** Encrypted backups are rotated and destroyed on a regular schedule

## 9. International Data Transfers

- Your data may be processed in the region where our infrastructure is hosted
- When data crosses borders, we use appropriate safeguards (standard contractual clauses, adequacy decisions, or equivalent protections)


## 10. Changes to This Policy

- We'll update this policy as our practices evolve. 
- For **material changes**, we'll notify you through the app and may require re-acceptance
